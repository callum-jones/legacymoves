About Sara
Sara Milne Rowe
Author · Performance Coach

Sara is the person leaders turn to when they need to cross a frontier they cannot alone.

Sara's work does not just change what you do. It changes who you are.

For more than 25 years, Sara has worked with the world's senior leaders at their most defining moments.

Her SHED Method and Coaching Impact method work precisely at this deeper level, helping leaders hear beneath their own expertise and conditioning, and find the clarity that only comes from there.

Legacy Moves® is the most personal work Sara does. It is the room where the question she has been holding with leaders for 25 years finally gets the time and space it deserves.
