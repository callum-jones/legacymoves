The Invitation

Legacy Moves is
a year of ignition

Eight leaders. One year together. Sara holds the space, with the warmth, directness, and instinct for what needs to be heard, drawing from insights from coaching conversations with Fortune 500 CEO C-suite leaders over the past 25 years.

Legacy Moves is intimate by design, it is a programme you are invited to join.

It is a decision to stop — deliberately and with care — to ask: what is mine to do that only I can do and what moves do I need to make now?

The Shape of the Year

One year invested in what matters most

01
The Ignition Session

A 90 minute coaching session to articulate and acknowledge what you want to create. Where the real work begins on your own Legacy Moves® journey.

02
The First Gathering

The first dinner, when you realise you're not alone. Eight leaders, facilitated and hosted by Sara to discuss the journey ahead, establish the relationships and trust that will sustain the work, and build fellowship.

03
Fuelling the flame

The moment your legacy becomes visible. Four days that will change the way you see your life, a deep immersion together, designed to ignite your legacy. Naming the flame that is genuinely yours, created from the clearest place you have yet found.

04
The First Return

A month on, the eight back together, face to face. A moment to celebrate and sustain what was lit.

05
Staying Lit

Two individual follow-up coaching sessions with Sara to consolidate and deepen the commitment made and the work begun.

06
Sustaining the Flame

Five months of virtual sessions with your Legacy Moves® community to sustain momentum, track progress and deepen your legacy project.

07
Tending the Fire

An evening back in the room. A talk to bring fresh thinking, followed by conversation, offering everyone the opportunity to reconnect with an element of the work and with each other. A moment to take stock together and return to the flame.

08
The Fellowship

A group of carefully curated leaders that stays with you long after the year ends, people who will walk with you for the rest of your life.

Membership of the Legacy Moves® Fellowship, connecting you to a growing network of leaders committed to leading and living with purpose.

Intimate by design. Eight leaders, one year together.
