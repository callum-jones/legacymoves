Who Legacy Moves Is For

Two kinds of leaders. One threshold.

You arrive at Legacy Moves when you are defining the next stage of your life and the impact you want to make.

Still in Role

The leader who knows they have more to bring

You are successful by every external measure. The career you built is significant and yet something in you is asking, "what else can your leadership bring?" You are in a position of genuine influence and you want to use it more intentionally, expanding what's already working and reaching for a deeper register of what you love doing.

"I am aware that success is no longer answering the question I am asking."

"I am in a position of real influence and I want to optimise it."

"The question isn't 'what's next?'. It's 'what's possible here?'"

Beyond the Role

The leader in the space between what was and what is next

The structure of corporate life had its own momentum. Without it you find yourself in new territory. You have wisdom, strong relationships, deep experience and a growing clarity that it is time to use all of it for something that is genuinely yours. You are at a threshold and know that the next phase is yours to write with intention.

"I know that I am more powerful than I thought and have more to give."

"So much more is possible and I want to create a positive impact."

Whatever you think is possible, Legacy Moves helps you name it, pressure test it and act on it.
