The Fellowship

The year ends. The fellowship doesn't.

The Legacy Moves® Fellowship is a community of leaders and changemakers building legacies that matter, who spark each other and open doors for each other — from across industries, geographies and generations. Leaders from previous groups remain connected to each other, to the work and to the questions that brought them here. When your year of ignition ends, the connection continues.

The Fellowship is defined not by what its members have achieved but by their depth of purpose and their appetite for more meaning, more impact, more alignment. And ultimately, by who they are committed to becoming.

"The fellowship is a living, growing movement of extraordinary people, across years and cohorts, stepping into a life with more impact."

Sara Milne Rowe
