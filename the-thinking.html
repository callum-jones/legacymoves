The Thinking

A Third System

In her book, The SHED Method, Sara Milne Rowe describes two ways the mind operates. There is the fast system, what she calls the Reptile and Dog Brains which run on instinct, urge and intuition reacting before we are conscious something has happened. And then there is the slower system, the rational Human Brain, that analyses, weighs and makes conclusions.

Daniel Kahneman spent decades mapping the same terrain and, in Thinking Fast and Slow, named them System 1 and System 2.

After so many years of working with leaders, Sara has come to the conclusion that there is a third system sitting beneath both the reactive and the rational. It is a deeper knowing that arrives as a pull, a quiet insistence, a calling. Many busy, successful leaders have lost touch with it, buried under the noise of the other two systems. You may have felt its pull without having a name for it.

The System Breakdown
System 1
Fast · Instinctive · Reactive
The gut response. Thousands of daily micro-decisions, essential for you to survive
System 2
Slow · Analytical · Deliberate
The rational mind. Here to help you thrive
System 3
Deep · Emergent · Knowing
Deeper, not faster or slower, than the other two. The knowing that is awake in you before words can express them. What will make you come alive
