What if the most important work of your life hasn't happened yet?

You have built something real: a career, a reputation, a way of operating in the world. And yet, there is a question underneath the success that success itself has never quite answered. You are not here because something is wrong. You are here because something is ready — and some part of you already knows it is time to act.
